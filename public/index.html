Director Profile

Where Cinematic Vision Meets E-Commerce Conversion

I am Oluwabiyi Ayodele Samson, a Nigerian Creative Director dedicated to solving one critical challenge for e-commerce brands: creating videos that don't just look stunning, but aggressively drive revenue.

A Decade of Commercial Leadership

Over the past 10+ years, Nigeria's e-commerce landscape has transformed dramatically. As online consumer attention spans shortened to seconds, standard promotional clips stopped converting.

My methodology blends high-end cinematic production values (RED/ARRI cameras, professional lighting, bespoke art direction) with relentless direct-response ad psychology: scroll-stopping visual hooks, emotional problem agitation, unboxings that trigger desire, and friction-free calls-to-action.
